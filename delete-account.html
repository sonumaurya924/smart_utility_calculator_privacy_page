Delete Your PaisaFlow Account

At PaisaFlow, we respect your privacy and give you full control over your account and personal data. If you no longer wish to use PaisaFlow, you can permanently delete your account and associated data.

Important:
Deleting your account is permanent and cannot be undone.

How to Delete Your Account

  1. Open the PaisaFlow app.
  2. Sign in to your account.
  3. Go to Profile → Settings → Delete Account.
  4. Read the confirmation message.
  5. Tap Delete Account.
  6. Confirm your request.

What Data Will Be Deleted?

When you delete your account, the following information will be permanently removed:

  • Your account profile.
  • Your expense records.
  • Your budget information.
  • Your wealth and financial data.
  • Your app preferences associated with your account.

Data That May Be Retained

Some limited information may be retained for up to 30 days to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, or maintain system security. After this period, the retained data will be permanently deleted unless a longer retention period is required by law.
